Four doors into the PPDS

The services answer different levels of the same question: monitor the market, discover the available data, interrogate it precisely, or navigate the relationships behind a procurement procedure.

Monitor

Dashboard

Indicators, trends, maps and visual reports for fast monitoring and policy analysis.

Discover

Data Catalogue

Find datasets, publishers, formats and metadata; understand what exists before analysing it.

Interrogate

Query Service

Start with prepared templates or create custom SPARQL queries for precise analytical questions.

Navigate

Graph Explorer

Follow notices, procedures, buyers, suppliers and their relationships in an interactive graph view.

Artificial Intelligence in the PPDS

The AI understands what the data means

Concepts and their relationships are defined explicitly, so the AI doesn't have to guess what a field or a value means.

Fewer hallucinations

Answers come from structured, verified facts rather than the model's general knowledge.

More accurate queries

The ontology guides the AI to build correct queries and can automatically reject ones that don't make sense.

It can answer complex questions

Explicit links between buyers, procedures, lots and winners let the AI connect information across many records.

Answers can be explained

Each answer can be traced back to its source data and the logic behind it, so people can check and trust it.
